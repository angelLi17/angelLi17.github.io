### GrapeSense

Built a multimodal image-to-image diffusion pipeline using SAM2, Flux and IP-Adapter to generate high-feature training data of diseased grape crops in vineyard settings. Optimized, trained and analyzed a YOLOv11 model for grape disease classification, evaluating it with mAP@50, confusion matrices and loss curves. Presented our collaborative research paper at the IEEE SmartAgri&SuSy Conference as first author, fielding audience questions and exchanging ideas with experts in the field.

### Cuneiform OCR

Developing an OCR and computer vision workflow to translate Assyrian cuneiform text.

### RedBean

A mobile app for studying alongside friends, with video calling, synchronized task lists and a Pomodoro timer. Built with the MVVM architecture to manage screen navigation and persistent data.

### ReStitch

A chatbot that suggests crafting ideas for upcycling old items, built with Retrieval Augmented Generation and NLP vector tokenization. Worked with my team through the full process: pitching, wireframing, developing and presenting the first iteration.
